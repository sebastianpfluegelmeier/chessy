How to play

  • A piece moves by the terrain it stands on — the destination's terrain matters next turn.
  • green = legal move, red = capture.
  • Sliding terrain shows movement rays; knights show jump targets.
  • Click any square to inspect its terrain pattern (ignores pieces).
  • Right-click or Esc to clear.
